## Changes shipped

### 1. Welcome screen (`src/game.ts`)

`drawWelcomeScreen()` renders a full-screen canvas overlay on every login:

- **First visit** (dayCount=0, no buildings): Lore intro — "You wash ashore on an island
  stripped bare…" — with key hints. Dismissed on any key or auto-fades after 320 frames.
- **Returning player**: "Welcome back, Guardian." — Day N · X tiles planted ·
  Biodiversity Y% · Z threats cleared. Auto-fades after 220 frames.

Initialized in `startGame()`. Any keydown dismisses it (fades out over ~20 frames).

### 2. Info guide panel (`src/game.ts`)

`drawInfoPanel()` — a 6-page paginated canvas panel accessed via `?` key or the new `[?]`
HUD button (above the existing `[H]` button).

Pages:
1. **Story & Eras** — premise, 3 eras, escape goal
2. **Characters** — Maya/Ren/Lena quests, Spirit Fox, Ghost Ship, Whale, Raiders
3. **Buildings & Items** — all 10 buildings + 5 items with costs and effects
4. **Tech & Combat** — 3 techs, gathering bonus stacking table, combat damage table
5. **Threats** — all 13 real-world atrocities with HP and shocking stat (split Era I/II)
6. **World & Ecology** — Day/Night, Seasons, Fog, Spirit Realm, ecological roles, zone stability

Navigate with `←` `→` arrow keys. Esc to close. Dismisses welcome screen when opened.

### 3. Ghost ship interaction (`src/game.ts`)

New `ghostShipInteracted` runtime flag (resets each drift cycle when ship wraps around).

In the E-key handler: if ghost ship is on-screen (`ghostShipX` 5 < x < WORLD_W-5) AND the
player is standing within 2.5 tiles of their Signal Fire, pressing E triggers a 5-line
dialog and drops +12 coin + 8 food. Resets when ship drifts off the far edge.

### 4. Per-threat loot (`src/game.ts`)

`playerAttack()`: replaced generic 30W+15S+10C+20CC drops with a per-threat loot table
matching each threat's `lootDescription`:
- Logging, hunting, pigeon: wood/stone loot
- Whaling, trawling, pesticide, pigeon: food loot
- Palm oil, sharks, ivory, rhino, exotic trade, plastics: coin loot
- Habitat fragmentation: stone loot
Carbon Credits still granted on all defeats (15–25 CC depending on threat).

### 5. spawnRaid() origin from live camps (`src/game.ts`)

Raiders now spawn from a randomly chosen live threat camp's `(tx, ty)` instead of the
hardcoded `CAMP_X=5, CAMP_Y=8`. Daytime retreat in `updateRaiders()` also updated to route
each raider toward the nearest live camp, not the hardcoded position.

### 6. Tree nursery gate for flagship species (`src/game.ts`)

`plantSpecies()`: species with `carbonCost >= 10` (coastal_redwood, brazil_nut, etc.) now
require at least one Tree Nursery built — return false if none exists. `drawPlantPanel()`
shows "🌱 Nursery required" in amber for those species instead of a cost button.

### 7. Whale education popup (`src/game.ts`)

When the whale surfaces (once per session), `educationPopup` is triggered with a 5-line
fact about whale carbon sequestration and the 3 million whales killed in the 20th century.

### 8. Archery arrow visualization (`src/game.ts`)

In `drawUnits()`: when a soldier is in `attacking` state, archery is researched, and the
target is beyond melee range (> 1.5 tiles), a faint amber arrow line is drawn from the
soldier to the target entity, with a small dot at the impact point.

### 9. HUD button updates (`src/game.ts`)

- `[H]` button label changed from `?` to `H` (was always the help key, now matches)
- Active state: `[H]` and `[?]` buttons highlight when their panel is open
- `[?]` button added above `[H]`, triggers info guide panel
