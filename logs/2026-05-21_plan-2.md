# Faraway v4 — Plan: Logic Fixes + Inspiration Dive

Date: 2026-05-21

## Goal
Fix all identified logic gaps, then weave in headline mechanics from Minecraft, AoE, Zelda,
Don't Starve, Stardew, Hollow Knight, Subnautica, FTL so the game feels like a recognisable
blend — "oh that's the AoE part, oh that's the Don't Starve bit, wait — this cave is Hollow Knight".

---

## Phase 1 — Logic fixes (this session)

### 1.1 Buildings actually do things
- [ ] Shelter: press E to Rest → restores HP + Essence fully; sets respawn point
- [ ] Workshop: opens tech panel (T key) with 3 researchable upgrades
- [ ] Forge: already gives +50% gather — add visual indicator on node tooltips
- [ ] Signal Fire: once built, ghost ship becomes interactable (radio distress signal)
- [ ] Dock: already has ship craft — add build progress bar on building sprite

### 1.2 Real combat
- [ ] Player HP (100), shown as red bar next to essence bar
- [ ] Wolves/raiders deal 10 HP damage on contact (replaces pure essence drain)
- [ ] Space bar = attack swing (1.5 tile radius, 25 damage, 0.5s cooldown, costs 0 essence)
- [ ] Attack arc indicator (flash sector on swing)
- [ ] Death: fade to black, respawn at Shelter (or spawn point), lose 20% resources
- [ ] HP restores at Shelter (E to rest, +50 HP + full essence)

### 1.3 Enemy dark camp + raids
- [ ] Enemy camp at (5, 8) — dark stone circle, visible from start
- [ ] Camp has 400 HP, destructible by player + soldiers
- [ ] Night raid cycle: every in-game night, spawns 2 raiders that path toward buildings
- [ ] Raiders: same as wolves visually but with armor tint, have 40 HP
- [ ] Raids escalate: each dawn that passes adds 1 extra raider per night (cap 6)
- [ ] Camp destroyed → no more raids; drops 30W 20S 10C loot pile
- [ ] Warning banner: "Raiders approaching from the northwest"

### 1.4 Hunger / urgency
- [ ] Hunger bar (0–100), shown next to HP
- [ ] Depletes 1/min real time, halved when resting
- [ ] Below 30: player slows 25%
- [ ] Below 10: HP drains 1/s
- [ ] Restore: press E near any food node when not depleted (eat in-place +20 hunger)
- [ ] Shelter also restores hunger to 80 when resting

### 1.5 Fog of war
- [ ] Grid of 48×36 booleans: explored[y][x]
- [ ] Tile is explored if player has been within 5 tiles
- [ ] Unexplored tiles: draw with heavy dark overlay (not pitch black — silhouette visible)
- [ ] Revealed permanently (saved in save.explored: number[] run-length encoded)
- [ ] Enemy camp tile always visible (it looms)

## Phase 4 — Headline inspiration features (this session or next)

### Minecraft feel
- [ ] Crafting grid: Workshop opens a 3×3 grid; recipes produce items (axe → +100% wood, pickaxe → +100% stone)
- [ ] Items go in inventory (I key, 9-slot hotbar at bottom)
- [ ] Equipping axe → chopping speed doubles, sound changes

### Don't Starve feel
- [ ] Sanity bar (alongside HP/Hunger) — depletes at night near wolves, restores near fire/shrine
- [ ] Below 20 sanity: shadows appear at screen edges, controls briefly invert

### Zelda feel
- [ ] Cave has 3 floors; each floor has locked door (needs key found on floor)
- [ ] Boss on floor 3: Giant Shadow Wolf (400 HP), drops the Cave Rune
- [ ] Earth Pulse breaks cracked walls (already have it) → reveals hidden rooms with loot

### Don't Starve + Stardew
- [ ] Seasons: Summer / Autumn / Winter / Spring (each lasts 1 real minute for now)
- [ ] Winter: food nodes frozen (0 yield), trees drop 50% wood, blizzard atmosphere
- [ ] Autumn: bonus wood (+30%), mushroom nodes appear (cook at Shelter for +hunger)
- [ ] Spring: all nodes yield double for first 30s, flowers bloom (aesthetic)

### FTL feel
- [ ] Random events on a timer: "A storm damaged your Workshop" / "Wolves stole 10 Wood overnight"
- [ ] Events require decisions: spend resources to fix, or leave it degraded

### Stardew Valley
- [ ] 3 NPC survivors appear after era II; each gives a quest and teaches a skill
- [ ] Maya the Fisher: teaches Fishing Mastery (+50% food) after you bring her 20 food
- [ ] Ren the Smith: teaches Forging (+1 weapon level) after you bring 15 stone
- [ ] Lena the Healer: creates medicine (craft at workshop, heals 50HP)

### Subnautica
- [ ] After escaping, ocean map unlocks: 5 offshore islands discoverable by sailing
- [ ] Each island has unique resource (rare minerals, blueprint fragments)
- [ ] Deep water (50+ tiles from shore) spawns Sea Serpent that chases the ship

### Hollow Knight
- [ ] Cave floor 2 has a merchant (Pale Being) who sells upgrades for Coin
- [ ] Upgrades: Max HP +25, Attack +15, Gather speed +25%
- [ ] Cave floor 3 boss drops "Soul Shard" — equipping it makes spirit spells free
