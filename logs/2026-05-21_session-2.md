## Key design principles encoded in this session

- Each creature/species has found its place to exist. The game teaches what that place is.
- Ecological dependencies make every species matter: fig → wasp → fig. Fail one, lose both.
- The threats database uses the player's growing empathy for the species they've planted to
  make the facts about hunting/logging hit much harder.
- Biodiversity index grows slowly, compounds with keystone species — mirroring real trophic cascades.
- The player doesn't escape: they restore. Then they sail to restore somewhere else.
