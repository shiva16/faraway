# Faraway — Session Log 2026-05-21-1

## Feature: Spirit Realm + Living World (A+C blend)

**User request:** Add action, magic and atmosphere — blend Option A (Spirit Realm + spells) with Option C (living world creatures + events).

---

## What was built

### Living World (Option C)
- **Deer** — 5 deer wander grass tiles; scatter/flee when player within 4 tiles
- **Birds** — 3 flocks of 5 near forest edge; take flight when player approaches
- **Ghost Ship** — semi-transparent silhouette drifts offshore on fog days only
- **Lightning** — random flash event every ~5 min (screen white pulse + Web Audio thunder)
- **Whale breach** — once per session; offshore south, arc animation + splash particles

### Spirit Realm (Option A)
- **F key** — toggles spirit mode with animated indigo overlay; water tiles glow luminescent
- **4 Runes** — Fire (ruins), Water (tide pool), Earth (hilltop), Wind (grove); only visible/collectible in spirit mode; each unlocks a spell + discovery entry
- **Spirit Fox** — visible at night or in spirit mode; wanders forest, leads toward nearest uncollected rune
- **Shadow Wolves** (2) — emerge from cave mouth at night; hunt player; drain Essence on contact; visible in both modes

### Spells (1–4 keys after rune collected)
| Spell       | Cost | Effect |
|-------------|------|--------|
| Fire Orb    | 20   | Projectile in facing direction, destroys wolf on hit |
| Water Veil  | 15   | 4s protective ring; wolves cannot enter 3-tile radius |
| Earth Pulse | 25   | Instant knockback to all wolves within 5 tiles |
| Wind Step   | 10   | 3s speed ×2.5; wolf collision ignored |

### Essence bar
- Max 100; shown bottom-center with rune spell icons
- Restores 0.08/frame standing still on grass/sand/path
- Drained –0.4/frame by wolf contact
- Spell icons dim when not enough essence to cast

### UI
- Spirit mode banner (top-center, animated alpha)
- Wolf proximity red vignette at night
- Essence flash (red screen) on wolf hit
- Lightning flash overlay
- Whale breach with splash particle animation
- Ghost ship alpha animation

## AoE Foundation (same session — v3)

**Resources**: Wood/Stone/Food/Coin gathered by pressing E near nodes on the map. Forge building gives +50% yield.

**Buildings** (B key → click-to-place on any passable tile):
- Shelter (20W) — rest spot
- Workshop (30W 10S) — required for era II
- Forge (20S 10C) — +50% gathering
- Signal Fire (15W) — visual beacon
- Dock (40W 20S) — ship assembly point

**Ship crafting**: approach Dock → E → craft Hull/Sail/Compass from resources → Set Sail

**Era system**: Stranded → Settled (Shelter+Workshop built) → Ready to Sail (all ship parts)

**Escape**: Fade + victory screen with stats; R to sail back, N for new game

**AoE roadmap noted**: Next sessions → Units+Combat, Enemy civ, Minimap, Fog of War, Ages, Tech tree, Quests, Inventory/Weapons
