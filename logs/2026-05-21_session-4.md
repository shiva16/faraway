## Changes shipped

### 1. Carbon costs on all 9 flagship species (`src/botany.ts`)

Added `carbonCost` to all species that lacked it:
- sycamore_fig: 8 CC
- baobab: 8 CC
- english_oak: 6 CC
- scots_pine: 5 CC
- coastal_redwood: 15 CC
- brazil_nut: 10 CC
- wild_olive: 5 CC

(banyan: 6 CC and kapok: 5 CC were already added in session 3)

### 2. Carbon credits now actually spendable (`src/game.ts`)

- `plantSpecies()`: blocks planting flagship species if insufficient CC; deducts CC on success
- `drawPlantPanel()`: shows CC cost in purple if insufficient, green if affordable; label changes to `+Ncc`

### 3. Building sprites for 5 invisible buildings (`src/game.ts`)

All 5 buildings now have pixel-art sprites in `drawBuildingSprite()`:
- **tree_nursery** — glass-green greenhouse with seedling rows
- **seed_bank** — bunker vault with golden wheel/lock
- **ranger_station** — tall watchtower on stilts with lookout window
- **water_catchment** — raised cistern with animated water shimmer
- **myco_lab** — low dome with animated green mycelium glow vents

### 4. Era-based threat spawning (`src/game.ts`)

Added `THREAT_SPAWN_POSITIONS` (all 13 threats, 2 spawn positions each) and `maybeSpawnThreats()`:
- Called once per game day in `updateSurvival()`
- Era 1 threats spawn from day 3+; era 2 threats from day 8+
- 20% chance per eligible threat per day (one spawn per day max)
- Shows era banner when new threat detected
- Passability check prevents spawning in water/walls

### 5. Ranger Station combat bonus (`src/game.ts`)

`playerAttack()`: +10 damage when a ranger_station is within 6 tiles of player.

### 6. Water Catchment effects (`src/game.ts`)

- `gatherResource()`: food nodes near water_catchment get +30% yield
- `plantSpecies()`: aquatic species can now be placed on sand tiles when water_catchment is within 6 tiles (managed wetland planting)

### 7. Zone stability → passive income (`src/game.ts`)

Each THRIVING zone passively generates +2 food + 1 wood per game day (in GLV daily tick).

### 8. Trophic cascade notifications (`src/game.ts`)

- Added `ZoneStability` import and `prevZoneStability` map
- When a zone transitions from any state → thriving, `educationPopup` shows:
  - Keystone species that stabilised the zone
  - Up to 4 fauna species returning to the area

### 9. Tile color shift with forest maturity (`src/game.ts`)

In `drawForestLayer()`, before each plant sprite: draws a `rgba(20,70,10)` tint
at `maturity × 0.28` alpha. Bare tiles stay brown; fully mature forest tiles
deepen to dark green — the landscape visually transforms as you restore it.

### 10. Zone bonus on resource gathering (`src/game.ts`)

`gatherResource()`: resource nodes in THRIVING forest zones get +15% yield.
