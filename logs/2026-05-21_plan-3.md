# Faraway — Plan 3: Forest Restoration & Ecological Education

Date: 2026-05-21

## Vision

Central theme: **Forest Restoration as Resistance**.
You are a Forest Guardian on a devastated island. Humanity's extractive industry — logging camps,
poachers, trophy hunters — has stripped the land bare. You replant native species, restore
ecological dependencies, grow biodiversity, and push back the destruction.
Every seed is a choice. Every forest, a victory. Sail on to restore the next island.

The game teaches real botany, taxonomy, ecological dependencies, and documents real-world
environmental atrocities with facts and context. Players should feel moral clarity about
what humans have done — and real hope that restoration is possible.

## Phase 2 — Forest mechanics (this session)

### 2.1 Planting system
- P key: opens species catalog panel
- Click tile (when in plant mode): plants selected species
- Cost: seeds/food/coin depending on species rarity
- Visual: pixel art plant sprite on tile, grows over 3 seasons
- Season modifiers: spring doubles growth, winter halts tropical species

### 2.2 Biodiversity Index
- Calculated per forest zone (5×5 tile regions)
- Formula: unique species × keystone_multiplier × connectivity_bonus
- Global index = weighted average of all zones
- HUD: green bar top-center with percentage and a leaf icon
- Milestone events at 25% / 50% / 75% / 100%

### 2.3 Ecological dependencies in practice
- Adjacent nitrogen-fixers boost neighboring plant growth rate ×1.5
- Keystone species (Ficus, Oak, Baobab) attract animals when present
- Mycorrhizal hosts create underground network — trees within 5 tiles share health
- Pollinator-hosts (flowers, fruit trees) enable fruiting of dependent species
- Missing dependency slows growth, adds flavor text: "Needs pollinators nearby"

### 2.4 Forest spread
- Mature species with nearby compatible neighbors auto-spread 1 tile/season
- Wildlife corridors: two forest patches within 3 tiles → connectivity bonus
- "Mother trees" (oldest of a species) have larger spread radius

- Current camp at (5,8) renamed: "Logging Operation"

- New camps generated at era thresholds: Poacher Den, Trophy Hunter Camp

### 3.2 New building types (ecological stations)
- Tree Nursery — grows seedlings; enables rare species
- Seed Bank — stores species from depleted zones; prevents extinction
- Ranger Station — trains Forest Rangers (renamed soldiers)
- Water Catchment — unlocks wetland species; boosts food nodes
- Mycorrhizal Lab — extends underground network range
